Task 1 General Training — письма (2026)

  1. Formal. You use your local library regularly. Write a letter to the librarian. Say what you like about the library, describe something you are not satisfied with, and suggest improvements.{{E:g1}}
  2. Formal. You want to rent a car for a holiday. Write to the rental agency. Explain what type of car you need, give the dates, and ask about the cost and conditions.{{E:g2}}
  3. Formal. You bought some clothes online but were not satisfied. Write to the company. Describe what you bought, explain the problem, and say why you need a replacement urgently.{{E:g3}}
  4. Formal. You are due to move into a rented apartment but need to delay. Write to the landlord. Explain why, say when you can move in, and ask what can be arranged.{{E:g4}}
  5. Semi-formal. Write to your manager asking for a salary increase. Describe your current role, explain why you deserve the increase, and say what you plan to do in future.{{E:g5}}
  6. Informal. You have recently moved to a new home. Write to an English-speaking friend. Explain why you moved, describe the new place, and invite them to visit.{{E:g6}}
  7. Informal. A friend has got engaged. Write to them. Say how you found out, how you feel about the news, and suggest meeting to celebrate.{{E:g7}}
  8. Informal. You are moving away. Write to your neighbours to say goodbye, thank them for their help, and say how to stay in touch.{{E:g8}}
  9. Informal. Invite a friend to a photography exhibition: say what it is, why they would enjoy it, and suggest arrangements.{{E:g9}}

Тон: formal — Dear Sir or Madam … Yours faithfully (имя неизвестно) или Dear Mr Brown … Yours sincerely; informal — Hi Anna … Best wishes / Take care. Каждому из трёх пунктов — свой абзац.

Как работать с образцами

У каждого вопроса Task 2 и у писем ниже есть образец уровня Band 8 с планом, полезной лексикой и разбором. Порядок: 5 минут на план → 35 минут на эссе от руки или в редакторе без проверки орфографии → открой образец и сравни структуру, позицию и связки. Не заучивай тексты целиком: экзаменаторы замечают заготовки. Забирай идеи, конструкции и выражения.

Task 2 — недавно попадалось на экзамене (с датами)

  1. Museums and historical sites are mainly visited by tourists rather than local people. Why is this the case? What can be done to attract more local people? (24 окт и 3 ноя 2026 · causes + solutions){{E:1}}
  2. Some people think that languages should be taught in small classes, while others believe that the number of students in a class does not matter. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. (30 сен, 14 окт, 4 ноя 2026 · discussion){{E:2}}
  3. The extinction of some animal species, as happened with dinosaurs and dodos, is a natural process, and people should not try to prevent it. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (7 сен, 2 ноя 2026 · opinion){{E:3}}
  4. Because people around the world watch the same films and television programmes and use the same media, countries are becoming more and more alike. Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages? (21 авг, 28 окт 2026 · advantages / disadvantages){{E:4}}
  5. In many countries, fewer and fewer people use bicycles as a means of transport. Why is this happening? What could be done to encourage people to cycle more? (27 авг, 21 окт 2026 · causes + solutions){{E:5}}
  6. Some people believe that the best way to improve road safety is to raise the minimum legal age for driving cars and motorbikes. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (31 июл 2026 · opinion){{E:6}}

  7. It is important to give children the chance to act and make decisions independently. To what extent do you agree or disagree?{{E:13}}

  5. Advertisements, whether amusing or annoying, do not really influence what people buy. To what extent do you agree or disagree?{{E:18}}

Environment & transport

  1. Some cities have vehicle-free days, when private cars are banned from the centre. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?{{E:19}}

  3. A great deal of food is wasted around the world. Why does this happen? How can the problem be solved?{{E:21}}
  4. Some people believe it is impossible to be a responsible tourist who respects the local culture and environment. To what extent do you agree or disagree?{{E:22}}
  5. Organised tours to remote places and communities are increasingly popular. Is this a positive or negative development for the local people and the environment?{{E:23}}
  6. Some people think animals may be used in any way that benefits humans; others think this is wrong. Discuss both views and give your opinion.{{E:24}}

Society, culture & technology

  1. Some people think that anyone can create art, while others believe it requires special talent. Discuss both views and give your opinion.{{E:25}}
  2. Some people think governments should fund artists such as painters and musicians; others think this is a waste of public money. Discuss both views and give your opinion.{{E:26}}
  3. Live performances such as theatre and concerts are no longer necessary because people can watch them on television or online. To what extent do you agree or disagree?{{E:27}}
  4. More and more knowledge is stored on the internet instead of in books. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?{{E:28}}
  5. The number of crimes committed by young people is rising in many countries. Why is this? How should young offenders be dealt with?{{E:29}}

Как писать Task 2: схемы по типам

ТипКак узнатьСтруктура (4 абзаца)
OpinionTo what extent do you agree or disagree?Intro + чёткая позиция → Довод 1 + пример → Довод 2 (или уступка + опровержение) → Вывод
DiscussionDiscuss both views and give your opinionIntro + твоя позиция → Взгляд A → Взгляд B (тот, с которым согласен, — вторым) → Вывод
Advantages / disadvantagesDo the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?Intro + ответ «да/нет» → Более слабая сторона → Более сильная сторона → Вывод
Causes + solutionsWhy…? What can be done?Intro → 2 причины → 2 решения (связаны с причинами) → Вывод
Two-part / positive–negativeДва прямых вопросаIntro (ответ на оба) → Ответ на вопрос 1 → Ответ на вопрос 2 → Вывод

Фразы

  • Intro: It is often argued that… / There is an ongoing debate about whether… / In recent years, … has become increasingly common.
  • Позиция: I firmly believe that… / While I accept that…, I would argue that… / In my view, the benefits clearly outweigh the drawbacks.
  • Доводы: The main reason for this is that… / A further point is that… / This is largely because…
  • Пример: For instance, … / A clear illustration of this is… / This can be seen in…
  • Уступка: Admittedly, … / It is true that… However, … / Although some would claim that…, this ignores the fact that…
  • Вывод: In conclusion, although…, I am convinced that… / To sum up, …

Частые ошибки, которые стоят баллов

  • Ответ не на все части вопроса (например, обсудил только одну точку зрения) — потолок 5.0 за Task Response.
  • Меньше 250 слов; заученные «шаблонные» абзацы не по теме.
  • Позиция меняется от вступления к выводу.
  • Списки доводов без развития: лучше 2 идеи с объяснением и примером, чем 5 коротких.
