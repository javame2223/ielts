## Work or Study | core
Q: Are you working or studying? | I'm a second-year student at a university in Tashkent, majoring in economics. I also do a bit of private tutoring at weekends, so I suppose I'm doing a little of both.
Q: Why did you choose your subject? | Mainly because I've always been good with numbers, and economics seemed more practical than pure maths. I also liked the idea that it explains everyday things, like why prices go up.
Q: Do you enjoy it? | On the whole, yes. Some of the theory is quite dry, to be honest, but I really enjoy the seminars where we argue about real cases.
Q: Do you prefer studying in the morning or afternoon? | Definitely the morning. My mind is much fresher before lunch, whereas by mid-afternoon I find myself reading the same paragraph three times.
Q: What technology do you use when studying? | Mostly my laptop, for writing essays and looking things up. I also use a flashcard app on my phone to revise vocabulary whenever I'm on the bus.
V: major in = специализироваться на; dry = сухой, скучный; on the whole = в целом; look things up = искать информацию; revise = повторять
N: Обрати внимание на приём «ответ + причина + деталь». Фразы to be honest, I suppose, on the whole делают речь естественной и работают на Fluency.

## Hometown | core
Q: Where is your hometown? | I come from Samarkand, which is in the central part of Uzbekistan, about three hundred kilometres from the capital.

N: Present Perfect (have opened, has been restored, it's lost) — стандартный способ говорить об изменениях. Подставь свой город.
