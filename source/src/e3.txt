E: The diagram illustrates the process by which used glass bottles are recycled.
E: Overall, there are eight stages in the process, beginning with the collection of empty bottles and ending with the sale of new ones. The process is cyclical, because the new bottles can eventually be recycled again.
E: At the first stage, used glass bottles are left in bottle banks, from which they are collected and transported by lorry to a recycling plant. There, the bottles are cleaned with high-pressure water before being sorted by colour into three groups: green, brown and clear glass. Each type of glass is then crushed into small pieces, known as cullet.
E: In the next step, the cullet is mixed with sand, soda ash and limestone and melted in a furnace at a temperature of 1,500°C. The liquid glass is poured into moulds to form new bottles, which are left to cool. Finally, the bottles are filled with drinks and delivered to shops, where they are bought by consumers, and the cycle begins again.

E: The diagram shows how a pumped-storage hydroelectric power station generates electricity, and how it operates differently during the day and at night.
E: Overall, the system works in two phases. During the day, water flows downhill from a high reservoir to a low one, producing electricity, while at night the same water is pumped back up so that the cycle can be repeated.
E: In the daytime, water is held behind a dam in the high-level reservoir. When electricity is needed, the gates of an intake are opened, and the water rushes down a large pipe to the power station. There, it drives reversible turbines, which turn generators to produce electricity. This electricity is then carried by power lines to the national grid, while the water flows into a low-level reservoir.
E: At night, when demand for electricity is lower, the process is reversed. Cheap electricity from the grid is used to run the turbines in the opposite direction, so that they work as pumps. As a result, water is pumped from the low-level reservoir back up to the high-level reservoir, ready to be used again the following day.
V: operates = работает, функционирует; works in two phases = работает в две фазы — главная мысль overview; flows downhill = течёт вниз по склону; producing electricity = вырабатывая электричество — -ing оборот-результат; so that = чтобы — цель; is held behind a dam = удерживается плотиной; When electricity is needed = когда нужно электричество; rushes down = устремляется вниз; drives = приводит в движение; to produce electricity = чтобы вырабатывать электричество; carried by power lines = передаётся по линиям электропередачи; demand for electricity = спрос на электричество; the process is reversed = процесс идёт в обратную сторону; in the opposite direction = в обратном направлении; ready to be used again = готовая к повторному использованию
N: 183 слова. Это не производство, а устройство, поэтому overview описывает не «этапы», а две фазы работы. Каждый абзац деталей = одна фаза, и в каждом есть связка времени (In the daytime / At night). Пассив чередуется с активом: вода «течёт» сама (flows, rushes), а люди и машины делают что-то с ней (is held, is pumped). Причинно-следственные связки (so that, As a result) показывают, как части системы связаны, — это поднимает Coherence.
