# 1
P: Тип: причины + решения. Вступление — перефраз и обещание двух частей. Абзац 1 — две причины (привычность; музеи рассчитаны на приезжих). Абзац 2 — два решения, каждое отвечает на свою причину (цены и часы; меняющаяся программа). Вывод.
E: In many cities, the people queuing outside famous museums and monuments are far more likely to be foreign visitors than residents. This essay will examine why local people tend to stay away and suggest how they might be encouraged to return.
E: There are two main reasons for this pattern. The first is simply familiarity. People who have grown up near a castle or gallery often feel they can go at any time, so the visit is postponed indefinitely, whereas a tourist with only three days in the city treats it as a priority. The second reason is that many such places are designed with outsiders in mind. Ticket prices are set at a level tourists will accept, permanent displays rarely change, and opening hours coincide with the working day. A resident who visited once on a school trip therefore has little incentive, and little opportunity, to go again.
E: Fortunately, both problems can be addressed. To begin with, institutions could offer residents free or heavily discounted entry, as several European cities already do, together with late opening on at least one evening a week so that working people can attend. In addition, museums need to give locals a reason to come back by staging temporary exhibitions, talks and family workshops that change regularly. If these events draw on the history of the surrounding community, for example by displaying residents' own photographs and stories, people are more likely to feel that the museum belongs to them rather than to visitors.
E: In conclusion, local people neglect their own heritage mainly because it is always available and seldom aimed at them. Lower prices, more convenient hours and a changing programme rooted in the community would go a long way towards bringing them back.
V: postpone indefinitely = откладывать на неопределённый срок; with outsiders in mind = в расчёте на приезжих; incentive = стимул; heavily discounted = с большой скидкой; go a long way towards = во многом способствовать
N: Обе части вопроса раскрыты в отдельных абзацах, а решения зеркально отвечают на причины — это и есть высокий Task Response. Связность держат The first… The second…, To begin with… In addition…. Вывод не добавляет новых идей.

E: Nevertheless, learning a language differs from learning history or mathematics because it is a skill that must be performed, not just understood. In a class of forty, each student may speak for less than a minute per lesson, which is nowhere near enough to develop fluency. In a group of ten, by contrast, everyone can take part in discussions and receive individual correction of their pronunciation and grammar. Shy learners, who would stay silent in a crowd, are also more willing to speak. My own experience supports this: I made more progress in three months in a small evening group than in several years of school lessons with thirty-five classmates.

P: Тип: agree / disagree. Позиция: не согласен. Абзац 1 — уступка: вымирание действительно бывает естественным. Абзац 2 — но нынешнее вызвано человеком и вредит нам самим. Вывод.
E: It is sometimes claimed that, since species have always died out, as the dinosaurs did, humans have no reason to interfere when animals disappear today. I disagree with this view, because the present wave of extinctions is neither natural nor harmless.
E: Admittedly, extinction has been a normal part of the history of life. Fossils show that the vast majority of species that ever existed vanished long before humans appeared, usually because the climate changed or a stronger competitor arrived. From this perspective, spending large sums to save every rare animal might seem to be a battle against nature itself, and it is true that resources for conservation are limited.
E: However, the comparison with the dinosaurs is misleading. Most animals that are endangered now are threatened not by natural processes but by human activity, such as the destruction of forests, pollution and illegal hunting. The dodo itself was wiped out by sailors and the animals they brought with them. If we are responsible for the damage, we surely have a duty to repair it. Moreover, protecting other species is in our own interest. Bees pollinate our crops, forests regulate the climate and many medicines are derived from wild plants. When one species disappears, others that depend on it may follow, and the consequences for agriculture and health can be serious.
E: In conclusion, while extinction can be a natural event, what we are witnessing today is largely of our own making and threatens the systems on which we depend. I therefore believe that people should make every reasonable effort to prevent it.
