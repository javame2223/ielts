Образец (Band 9), 178 слов

The line graph compares the proportions of Australian men and women aged 18 to 34 who were in work over a 35-year period from 1976 to 2011.

Overall, although men were more likely to be employed throughout the period, the gap between the sexes narrowed considerably, as the female rate rose steadily while the male rate fell slightly.

In 1976, almost nine in ten young men (89%) had a job, compared with just over half of young women (52%). Male employment then declined gradually to 84% in 1986 before dropping more sharply to a low of 78% in 1991. After that it recovered slowly, reaching 83% in 2006, and ended the period at 81%, eight percentage points below its starting level.

By contrast, the figure for women increased in every five-year period. It climbed from 52% to 63% by 1991 and continued to rise to 72% in 2006, before levelling off at 73% in 2011. As a result, the difference between the two groups shrank from 37 percentage points at the beginning to only 8 by the end.

Practice Task 1.6 — Process

The diagram below shows the process by which ceramic pots are manufactured. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
  1. Raw clay is dug out of the ground by an excavator and transported by lorry to the factory.
  2. The clay is crushed and passed through a sieve to remove stones.
  3. Water is added in a mixing tank; the liquid clay is poured into moulds.
  4. The moulds are left for 4–6 hours; the pots are then removed and smoothed by hand.
  5. Pots are placed in a drying room (2 days, 40°C).
  6. First firing in a kiln at 1,000°C.
  7. Pots are painted and coated with glaze.
  8. Second firing at 1,200°C; cooling for 24 hours.
  9. Quality check → packaging → delivery to shops.

Подсказка: в overview укажи число этапов и где процесс начинается и заканчивается (Overall, there are nine main stages, beginning with the extraction of clay and ending with the delivery of finished pots). Используй пассив и связки последовательности.
