# 18 | Reported Speech | 3 | Косвенная речь: сдвиг времён, местоимения и указатели времени; вопросы и просьбы; глаголы-«пересказчики» (claim, deny, suggest, warn)
@GOAL Передавать чужие слова, вопросы и просьбы грамматически точно и разнообразно (не только *said*), чтобы в Task 2 и Speaking ссылаться на мнения: *Experts claim that…, Some argue that…*
@PLAN
1. Said vs told; that можно опустить.
2. Сдвиг времён (backshift) и когда он не обязателен.
3. Местоимения, места и время: here → there, tomorrow → the next day.
4. Косвенные вопросы: asked if / where … (прямой порядок слов).
5. Просьбы и приказы: told / asked someone (not) to do.
6. Глаголы-пересказчики с разными конструкциями.
7. Практика 1–6 и тест по теме.
@THEORY
### 1. Say и tell
- **say** (something) (to someone): *She **said** (that) she was tired. She **said to me** that…*
- **tell** + **кому** обязательно: *She **told me** (that) she was tired.*
✗ She said me that… → ✓ She told me that… / She said to me that…
✗ He told that he was busy. → ✓ He said that he was busy.
### 2. Сдвиг времён
Если вводящий глагол в прошлом (*said, told, asked*), время обычно «отступает» на шаг назад:
| Прямая речь | Косвенная речь |
| "I **work** here." | He said he **worked** there. |
| "I **am working**." | He said he **was working**. |
| "I **have finished**." | She said she **had finished**. |
| "I **finished** yesterday." | She said she **had finished** the day before. |
| "I **will** call you." | He said he **would** call me. |
| "I **can** swim." | She said she **could** swim. |
| "I **must** go." | He said he **had to** go. (или must) |
| "You **should** rest." | She said I **should** rest. (не меняется: would, could, might, should, ought to) |
**Сдвиг не обязателен**, если сказанное всё ещё верно: *She said she **lives** in London* (до сих пор живёт), *The teacher said the Earth **goes** round the Sun.* Если вводный глагол в настоящем (*says, has said*), времена не меняются: *He **says** he **is** tired.*
### 3. Указатели
| Прямая | Косвенная |
| now | then, at that moment |
| today / tonight | that day / that night |
| yesterday | the day before, the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day, the following day |
| last week | the week before, the previous week |
| next month | the following month |
| ago | before / earlier |
| here / this | there / that |
### 4. Вопросы
- Общий вопрос → **if / whether** + прямой порядок: *"Are you ready?" → She **asked if I was** ready.*
- Специальный → вопросительное слово + прямой порядок, без do/did: *"Where do you live?" → He **asked me where I lived**.*
✗ He asked me where did I live. → ✓ He asked me where I lived.
✗ She asked was I hungry. → ✓ She asked if I was hungry.
### 5. Просьбы, приказы, советы
**tell / ask / advise / warn / remind / order + объект + (not) to + V**:
- *"Please close the door." → She **asked me to close** the door.*
- *"Don't be late!" → He **told us not to be** late.*
- *"You should see a doctor." → She **advised me to see** a doctor.*
### 6. Глаголы-пересказчики
| Конструкция | Глаголы | Пример |
| + that | claim, argue, explain, admit, deny, insist, complain, point out, warn, agree | *Experts **claim that** the policy will fail.* |
| + to-infinitive | agree, refuse, offer, promise, threaten | *He **promised to help**.* |
| + объект + to-infinitive | advise, ask, encourage, invite, persuade, remind, warn, urge | *She **reminded me to call**.* |
| + -ing | admit, deny, suggest, recommend | *He **denied taking** the money. She **suggested going** by train.* |
| + preposition + -ing | apologise for, accuse sb of, insist on, blame sb for, congratulate sb on | *He **apologised for being** late.* |
✗ He suggested me to go. → ✓ He suggested that I (should) go. / He suggested going.
✗ She explained me the rules. → ✓ She explained the rules to me.
@USE
### Task 2
Вместо «Some people say…» используй точные глаголы: *Supporters of this policy **argue that**… Critics **point out that**… Scientists **have warned that**… Some people **claim that**…, but I would argue that…*
### Speaking Part 2 / 3
Пересказ разговора делает историю живой: *My friend **told me** she **had** never **been** abroad, so I **suggested going** to Turkey together.*
