# 26 | Subjunctive | 4 | Сослагательное наклонение: suggest / insist / it is essential that he be; I wish / If only; were; It's time we…; as if; would rather you…; устойчивые выражения
@GOAL Правильно использовать сослагательные формы, которые делают письменную речь формальной и точной: *It is essential that every child **have** access…, The committee recommended that the law **be** changed, I wish I **had** started earlier.*
@PLAN
1. Что такое subjunctive в английском: «голая» форма глагола и were.
2. Mandative subjunctive: suggest, recommend, insist, demand, require that + V (без -s).
3. It is essential / vital / important / necessary that + V.
4. Британская альтернатива: should + V.
5. Wish и If only: настоящее, прошлое, would.
6. Were-subjunctive: if I were, as if / as though.
7. It's (high) time + Past Simple; would rather + Past Simple.
8. Устойчивые выражения: be that as it may, come what may, so be it, God save…
9. Практика 1–6 и тест по теме.
@THEORY
### 1. Mandative subjunctive (требование, предложение)
После глаголов **suggest, recommend, propose, insist, demand, request, require, urge, ask (formal), advise** + **that** — глагол в **базовой форме** для всех лиц: без *-s*, без прошедшего времени, *be* вместо *is/are/was*.
> The doctor **recommended that** he **stop** smoking. (не *stops*)
> They **insisted that** the meeting **be** postponed. (не *is / was*)
> I **suggest that** she **apply** for the scholarship.
> The law **requires that** every driver **have** insurance.
Отрицание — *not* перед глаголом: *We **suggest that** he **not** travel alone.*
✗ The teacher suggested that he studies harder. → ✓ The teacher suggested that he study harder. (или *should study*)
✗ They demanded that the manager was fired. → ✓ They demanded that the manager be fired.
### 2. It is + прилагательное + that
**essential, vital, crucial, important, necessary, imperative, desirable, advisable** + that + базовая форма:
> **It is essential that** every child **have** access to education.
> **It is vital that** the government **act** now.
> **It is important that** students **be** given feedback.
### 3. Британский вариант с should
В BrE часто: *The doctor recommended that he **should stop** smoking. It is essential that every child **should have**…* Оба варианта правильны на экзамене; «чистый» subjunctive звучит формальнее.
### 4. Wish и If only
| Время сожаления | Форма | Пример |
| о настоящем | wish + **Past Simple / were** | *I **wish** I **had** more free time. I **wish** I **were** taller.* |
| о прошлом | wish + **Past Perfect** | *I **wish** I **had studied** harder.* |
| раздражение / желание перемен (действие других) | wish + **would** | *I **wish** the neighbours **would** stop making noise.* |
| способность | wish + **could** | *I **wish** I **could** speak Chinese.* |
**If only** — то же, но эмоциональнее: ***If only** I **had listened** to you!*
✗ I wish I have a car. → ✓ I wish I had a car.
✗ I wish I would be rich. → ✓ I wish I were rich. (*would* не используется о себе и о состояниях)
✗ I wish I didn't go to the party. (о прошлом) → ✓ I wish I hadn't gone to the party.
### 5. Were-subjunctive
- *If I **were** you…, If she **were** here…* (тема 16)
- **as if / as though** (нереально): *He talks **as if** he **were** an expert* (но он не эксперт). *She looked **as though** she **had seen** a ghost.*
- Если сравнение возможно/реально, — обычное время: *It looks **as if** it **is** going to rain.*
### 6. It's time и would rather
- **It's (about / high) time + подлежащее + Past Simple** — давно пора: ***It's high time** the government **took** action.* (≠ *It's time to take action* — тоже верно, но без «упрёка»)
- **would rather + подлежащее + Past Simple** — предпочитаю, чтобы кто-то: *I'**d rather** you **didn't** tell anyone.* (для прошлого — Past Perfect: *I'd rather you **hadn't told** her.*)
### 7. Устойчивые формулы
***Be that as it may*** (как бы то ни было) — полезно в эссе; ***Come what may*** (что бы ни случилось); ***So be it*** (пусть так); ***God save the King***; ***Long live…***; ***Suffice it to say*** (достаточно сказать); ***If need be*** (если понадобится).
@USE
### Task 2
Subjunctive — элегантный способ формулировать рекомендации:
> ***It is essential that** governments **invest** in public transport.*
> ***I would suggest that** every school **provide** free meals.*
> ***It is high time** authorities **took** air pollution seriously.*
> ***Be that as it may**, I still believe that…*
### Letters (GT)
*I **request that** my deposit **be** returned. I would **suggest that** the meeting **be** moved to Friday.*
### Speaking
Part 3 о сожалениях и желаниях: *I **wish** schools **taught** more practical skills. **If only** I **had started** learning English earlier!*
