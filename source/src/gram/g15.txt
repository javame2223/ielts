# 15 | Gerund & Infinitive | 3 | -ing или to + инфинитив: глаголы, прилагательные, предлоги; stop/remember/try с разным смыслом; let/make; герундий как подлежащее
@GOAL Автоматически выбирать форму второго глагола (enjoy **doing**, decide **to do**, make someone **do**) и понимать, как меняется смысл после stop, remember, forget, try, regret, mean, go on. Использовать герундий-подлежащее для «академичного» стиля Task 2.
@PLAN
1. Глаголы + -ing.
2. Глаголы + to-infinitive.
3. Глагол + объект + to-infinitive (want someone to do).
4. Инфинитив без to: make, let, help, модальные; see / hear someone do / doing.
5. Глаголы с изменением смысла: stop, remember, forget, try, regret, mean, go on, need.
6. После предлогов — всегда -ing (включая look forward to, be used to, object to).
7. Прилагательные + to: easy to, difficult to; it is + adj + to.
8. Герундий как подлежащее и инфинитив цели (in order to).
9. Практика 1–6 и тест по теме.
@THEORY
### 1. Глагол + -ing
**enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, miss, suggest, recommend, consider, deny, admit, imagine, keep, practise, risk, involve, can't stand, can't help, give up, put off, carry on, look forward to, be worth, it's no use**.
> I **enjoy reading**. They **suggested taking** a taxi. It's **worth visiting** the old town.
✗ He suggested to go home. → ✓ He suggested going home. / He suggested that we (should) go home.
✗ I avoid to eat sugar. → ✓ I avoid eating sugar.
### 2. Глагол + to + инфинитив
**want, would like, decide, plan, hope, expect, agree, refuse, promise, offer, manage, fail, afford, learn, seem, appear, tend, choose, arrange, attempt, threaten, deserve, aim, intend**.
> We **decided to leave** early. Prices **tend to rise** in winter. Many families **can't afford to buy** a house.
### 3. Глагол + объект + to + инфинитив
**want, would like, expect, ask, tell, advise, allow, encourage, force, persuade, remind, teach, warn, enable, require, invite**:
> I **want you to help** me. Governments should **encourage people to use** public transport. Technology **enables us to work** from home.
✗ I want that you come. → ✓ I want you to come.
✗ The law allows to drive at 17. → ✓ The law allows people to drive at 17. / The law allows driving at 17.
### 4. Без to
- После модальных: *can do, must go, should study*.
- **make + объект + V**: *My parents **made me study**.* (в пассиве с to: *I **was made to study***)
- **let + объект + V**: *They **let us leave** early.*
- **help (to) + V**: *This **helps (to) reduce** costs.*
- **had better, would rather**: *You'd better go.*
- Восприятие: **see / hear / watch / notice + объект + V** (всё действие) или **+ V-ing** (часть процесса): *I **saw him cross** the road. I **saw him crossing** the road.*
### 5. Меняется смысл
| Глагол | + -ing | + to-infinitive |
| **stop** | прекратить: *He **stopped smoking**.* | остановиться, чтобы: *He **stopped to smoke**.* |
| **remember** | помнить то, что было: *I **remember meeting** her.* | не забыть сделать: ***Remember to lock** the door.* |
| **forget** | забыть, что было (обычно с never): *I'll **never forget seeing** the ocean.* | забыть сделать: *I **forgot to call** him.* |
| **try** | попробовать (эксперимент): ***Try drinking** some water.* | постараться (трудно): *I **tried to open** the window, but it was stuck.* |
| **regret** | жалеть о сделанном: *I **regret leaving** school early.* | с сожалением сообщать: *We **regret to inform** you…* |
| **mean** | означать, повлечь: *A new job **means moving** to another city.* | собираться: *I **meant to call** you.* |
| **go on** | продолжать то же: *She **went on talking**.* | перейти к другому: *He **went on to become** president.* |
| **need** | пассивное значение: *The car **needs washing**.* | активное: *I **need to wash** the car.* |
Глаголы **like, love, hate, prefer, start, begin, continue** принимают обе формы почти без разницы. Но *would like / would love / would prefer* + **to**: *I'**d like to go**.*
### 6. После предлогов — -ing
*interested **in learning**, good **at solving**, instead **of driving**, **by using**, **without asking**, **before leaving**, **after finishing**, responsible **for organising***.
Ловушка — **to** как предлог: *look forward **to seeing**, be used **to working**, get used **to living**, object **to paying**, be committed **to reducing**, contribute **to improving**, the key **to succeeding**, when it comes **to choosing***.
✗ I look forward to hear from you. → ✓ I look forward to hearing from you.
✗ We are committed to reduce emissions. → ✓ We are committed to reducing emissions.
### 7. Прилагательное + to-infinitive
*It is **difficult / important / essential / easy / necessary / possible** **to** find work.* *English is **easy to learn**. I was **pleased to hear** the news. She was **the first woman to win** the prize.*
### 8. Герундий-подлежащее и инфинитив цели
- Подлежащее-действие — герундий: ***Learning** a language **takes** time. **Recycling** reduces waste.* (глагол в ед. ч.)
- Цель — **to / in order to / so as to**: *Many people move to cities **(in order) to find** work.* ✗ *for find work* → ✓ *to find work*; *for + существительное*: *for work*.
- Отрицательная цель — **in order not to / so as not to**: *We left early **so as not to miss** the train.*
@USE
### Task 2
- Герундий в начале предложения звучит академично: ***Banning** cars from city centres **would** reduce pollution. **Investing** in education **benefits** everyone.*
- Глагол + объект + to-infinitive — готовые «решения»: *Governments should **encourage / require / force / allow** companies **to** …*; *This **enables people to** …*
- Цель через *in order to* разнообразит связки: *…in order to reduce traffic congestion.*
### Writing GT (письма)
- *I look forward **to hearing** from you. I would appreciate **receiving** a reply. I am writing **to complain** about… Would you mind **sending** me…? I would like **to request**…*
### Speaking
- Part 1 «Do you like…?» → *I really **enjoy cooking**, but I **can't stand washing** up.* — естественный «range».
