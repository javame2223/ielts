# 13 | Comparatives & Superlatives | 2 | Сравнительная и превосходная степень, as…as, twice as many as, the more…the more, much/far/slightly + comparative
@GOAL Сравнивать точно и разнообразно — главный навык Task 1 («make comparisons where relevant») и полезный приём в Task 2. Знать формы, усилители и конструкции с кратностью.
@PLAN
1. Образование: -er/-est и more/most; неправильные формы.
2. Сравнение двух: than; равенство: as … as, not as … as.
3. Усилители и смягчители: much, far, considerably, slightly, a little.
4. Кратность: twice as high as, three times more than, half as many as.
5. The more…, the more…; comparative and comparative.
6. Превосходная: the … of / in, one of the + мн. ч., the second largest.
7. Практика 1–5 и тест по теме.
@THEORY
### 1. Формы
| Прилагательное | Сравнительная | Превосходная |
| 1 слог: cheap, high | cheap**er**, high**er** | the cheap**est**, the high**est** |
| -e: large | larg**er** | the larg**est** |
| гласная + согласная: big, hot | bi**gger**, ho**tter** | the bi**ggest** |
| -y: easy, busy | eas**ier**, bus**ier** | the eas**iest** |
| 2+ слога: modern, expensive | **more** modern, **more** expensive | the **most** expensive |
| неправильные | good → **better**, bad → **worse**, far → **further/farther**, many/much → **more**, little → **less** | **the best, the worst, the furthest, the most, the least** |
Наречия: *quickly → more quickly; fast → faster; well → better; badly → worse*.
✗ more cheaper → ✓ cheaper ✗ the most biggest → ✓ the biggest ✗ more good → ✓ better
### 2. Than и as … as
- *Rents in London are higher **than** in Manchester.*
- Равенство: *The figure for 2010 was **as high as** in 2000.*
- Неравенство: *Trains are **not as** fast **as** planes.* (= planes are faster)
- После than / as — объектное местоимение или полное предложение: *She is taller **than me** / **than I am***.
- Сравни правильные вещи: ✗ *The population of Tokyo is larger than Paris* → ✓ *…larger than **that of** Paris* / *…than Paris's*.
### 3. Степень разницы
| Большая разница | Небольшая разница |
| **much / far / considerably / significantly / substantially** higher | **slightly / a little / marginally / a bit** higher |
✗ very higher → ✓ much higher ✗ more higher → ✓ much higher
### 4. Кратность (Task 1!)
- ***twice as** many tourists **as***, ***three times as** high **as***, ***half as** much **as***
- ***twice / three times** the number of*: *Japan had **twice the number of** visitors that Spain had.*
- *The figure **doubled / tripled / halved**.*
- *X was **three times higher than** Y* — разговорно допустимо, но *three times as high as* точнее.
### 5. Двойное сравнение и рост
- ***The more** you practise, **the better** you become.* ***The higher** the price, **the fewer** people buy it.*
- Постепенное изменение: *It's getting **colder and colder**. Life is becoming **more and more** expensive.*
### 6. Превосходная степень
- ***the** highest **in** the world / **in** the group*, ***the** most popular **of** the five activities*.
- ***one of the** most important **problems*** (мн. ч.!).
- ***the second** largest, **the third** most popular*.
- ***the least** popular* = самый непопулярный.
- С Present Perfect: *the best film I **have ever seen***.
IELTS: в Task 1 сравнение не «украшение», а требование. Чередуй: *higher than*, *the highest*, *twice as many as*, *slightly lower*, *the second most popular*, *by contrast*, *while*. Ошибка *more higher* или *the most biggest* сразу показывает уровень ниже 6.

London is ____ (expensive) than Manchester. | more expensive
This is ____ (good) restaurant in town. | the best

Which is ____ (large) city in Canada? | the largest
The situation is getting ____ (bad). | worse
This exercise is ____ (easy) than the last one. | easier
She is ____ (old) of the three sisters. | the oldest/the eldest

Walking is ____ (healthy) than driving. | healthier
That was ____ (bad) film I've ever seen. | the worst

Buses are not ____ trains. | A as fast as | B as faster as | C faster as | A
The figure for 2020 was ____ higher than in 2019, only by 1%. | A considerably | B slightly | C much | B
She is a little ____ than her brother. | A tall | B taller | C tallest | B
My brother earns more than ____. | A I | B me | C my | B
The climate of Spain is warmer than ____ of Britain. | A it | B that | C this | B
