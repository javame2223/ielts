# 27 | Advanced Sentence Structures | 4 | Номинализация, сложные именные группы, параллелизм, уступка (despite / whereas / much as / however + adj), приложение, эмфатическое do, эллипсис, whereby / thereby
@GOAL Собрать всё вместе: писать плотные академические предложения уровня Band 8–9 (*The rapid growth of online shopping has led to the closure of many high-street stores*), без ошибок в связках уступки и противопоставления и с параллельными конструкциями.
@PLAN
1. Номинализация: из глагола — существительное (grow → growth).
2. Сложные именные группы: определения до и после существительного.
3. Связки уступки и контраста: although, despite, in spite of, whereas, while, however, nevertheless, much as, adj + as/though.
4. Причина и результат внутри предложения: due to, owing to, as a result of, thereby, whereby, which means that.
5. Параллелизм.
6. Приложение (apposition) и вводные.
7. Эмфатическое do, fronting, эллипсис и замена (do so, one, so / not).
8. Как сочетать всё в одном абзаце.
9. Практика 1–6 и тест по теме.
@THEORY
### 1. Номинализация
Академический английский «упаковывает» действия в существительные:
| Глагольный стиль | Номинализация |
| Prices **rose rapidly**, and this **worried** consumers. | The **rapid rise** in prices **caused concern** among consumers. |
| If we **invest** more in schools, students **will achieve** more. | Greater **investment** in schools **will lead to** higher student **achievement**. |
| People **are using** cars more, so the air **is getting** dirtier. | The **increased use** of cars **has resulted in** a **deterioration** in air quality. |
Пары: *grow → growth, decide → decision, fail → failure, develop → development, reduce → reduction, introduce → introduction, consume → consumption, able → ability, available → availability, long → length, high → height*.
### 2. Именная группа
**определитель + наречие + прилагательное + существительное-определитель + ГЛАВНОЕ СУЩ. + предлог/причастие/придаточное**
> *the **rapidly growing** **online** **shopping** market **in Asia***
> *a **significant** **decrease** **in** the **number** **of** people **living alone***
✗ the increase of the population's growing → ✓ the increase in population growth / population growth
### 3. Уступка и контраст — что после связки?
| + предложение (подлежащее + глагол) | + существительное / -ing | Отдельное предложение / наречие |
| **although, though, even though, while, whereas** | **despite, in spite of** (+ the fact that + предложение) | **However,** … / **Nevertheless,** … / **Even so,** … / …, **though**. |
> ***Although** the city is expensive, many people move there.*
> ***Despite** the high cost / **Despite being** expensive, the city attracts many people.*
> *The city is expensive. **However**, many people move there.*
> ***Whereas** sales rose in Asia, they fell in Europe.* (сравнение двух фактов)
> ***Much as** I admire his work, I disagree with him.* (= хотя очень)
> ***Expensive as / though** it is, the city attracts many people.* (= хотя дорогой — эмфатично)
> ***However hard** they tried, they couldn't win.* (как бы ни)
✗ Despite it is expensive, … → ✓ Although it is expensive, … / Despite being expensive, …
✗ Although the price was high, but people bought it. → ✓ Although the price was high, people bought it.
✗ However the price was high, people bought it. → ✓ However, the price was high… / Although the price was high, …
### 4. Причина и результат
- **because of / due to / owing to / as a result of** + существительное: *Flights were cancelled **due to** bad weather.*
- **thereby + -ing** (тем самым): *The city banned cars, **thereby reducing** pollution.*
- **whereby** (посредством которого — формальное *by which*): *a system **whereby** students can borrow laptops*.
- **…, which means that** / **…, resulting in** / **…, leading to**.
### 5. Параллелизм
Однородные части — одной грамматической формы:
✗ The government should build schools, hospitals and improving roads. → ✓ …build schools and hospitals and improve roads.
✗ She likes swimming, to read and cooking. → ✓ She likes swimming, reading and cooking.
✗ The plan is cheap, efficient and it saves time. → ✓ The plan is cheap, efficient and time-saving.
С парными союзами: ***not only** X **but also** Y, **both** X **and** Y, **either** X **or** Y, **neither** X **nor** Y* — X и Y одного типа: ✗ *not only in Asia but also Europe* → ✓ *not only in Asia but also in Europe*.
### 6. Приложение и вводные
- *Tashkent, **the capital of Uzbekistan**, has a population of over three million.*
- *The proposal, **in my view**, is unrealistic.*
- *Coal — **once the main source of energy** — now accounts for only 10%.*
### 7. Эмфаза и экономия
- **Эмфатическое do**: *I **do** understand your concerns. Technology **does** have some drawbacks.* (усиливает уступку)
- **Fronting** (вынос вперёд): ***Particularly worrying is** the rise in youth unemployment. **More important than** money **is** health.*
- **Эллипсис / замена**: *Some countries have introduced the tax, and others will **do so** soon. "Will prices rise?" — "I think **so** / I hope **not**." The old system was slower than the new **one**.*
@USE
### Task 1 и Task 2 — один абзац «до» и «после»
До (Band 6): *Many people use cars. So there is a lot of pollution. The government should do something. It should build more bus lanes and it should make tickets cheaper.*
После (Band 8–9): ***The widespread use of private cars** has led to **a serious deterioration** in air quality. **Although** some measures have been introduced, **it is essential that** governments **go further**, **not only** expanding bus lanes **but also** making public transport more affordable, **thereby reducing** the number of vehicles on the roads.*
### Как тренировать
Возьми свой старый абзац и примени к нему три приёма: одну номинализацию, одну уступку (although / despite / whereas) и одно предложение с *thereby / which means that*. Не больше: перегруженный текст читается хуже.
