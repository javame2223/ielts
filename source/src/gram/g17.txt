@THEORY
### 1. Формула и времена
Пассив = **be** в нужном времени + **V3** (третья форма). Время показывает только *be*.
| Время | Актив | Пассив |
| Present Simple | They **make** cars here. | Cars **are made** here. |
| Present Continuous | They **are building** a bridge. | A bridge **is being built**. |
| Past Simple | They **built** it in 1900. | It **was built** in 1900. |
| Past Continuous | They **were repairing** the road. | The road **was being repaired**. |
| Present Perfect | They **have closed** the factory. | The factory **has been closed**. |
| Past Perfect | They **had sold** the house. | The house **had been sold**. |
| Future | They **will open** a museum. | A museum **will be opened**. |
| be going to | They **are going to demolish** it. | It **is going to be demolished**. |
| Modal | You **must wear** a helmet. | A helmet **must be worn**. |
| Modal Perfect | Someone **should have told** him. | He **should have been told**. |
| Infinitive / -ing | I want **someone to invite** me. / I hate **people laughing** at me. | I want **to be invited**. / I hate **being laughed** at. |
### 2. Когда пассив
- Исполнитель неизвестен, неважен или очевиден: *My bike **was stolen**. The criminal **was arrested**.*
- Важен процесс или результат (наука, производство, Task 1): *The clay **is heated** to 1,000°C.*
- Официальный, безличный стиль: *Applications **must be submitted** by Friday.*
- **by** + исполнитель — только если он важен: *Guernica **was painted by** Picasso.*
- Непереходные глаголы (*happen, arrive, die, rise, fall, occur, seem, exist, belong*) пассива **не имеют**.
✗ The accident was happened at night. → ✓ The accident happened at night.
✗ Prices were risen. → ✓ Prices rose. / Prices were raised (by the company).
✗ The museum built in 1890. → ✓ The museum was built in 1890.
✗ English is speaking in many countries. → ✓ English is spoken in many countries.
### 3. Два дополнения
*They gave **me** a prize.* → ***I was given** a prize.* (чаще) / *A prize **was given to me**.* Так же: *offer, send, show, tell, teach, promise, pay*.
### 4. Безличный пассив (say, believe, think, expect, report, know, consider)
| It + пассив + that | Подлежащее + пассив + to-infinitive |
| **It is said that** the castle is haunted. | The castle **is said to be** haunted. |
| **It is believed that** he lives abroad. | He **is believed to live** abroad. |
| **It is thought that** the fire started at night. | The fire **is thought to have started** at night. (прошлое → to have + V3) |
| **It is expected that** prices will rise. | Prices **are expected to rise**. |
Также: ***There is thought to be** a link between diet and cancer.*
### 5. Have / get something done
Кто-то делает для тебя: *I **had my hair cut**. We're **having the kitchen painted**. You should **get your eyes tested**.* Также о неприятностях: *She **had her phone stolen**.*
### 6. Get-пассив (разговорный)
*He **got injured** in the match. They **got married** last year. I **got caught** in the rain.* — В эссе лучше *be*.
@USE
### Task 1 — процессы и карты
- Процесс: почти все глаголы в пассиве, Present Simple: *First, the raw materials **are collected** and **taken** to the factory, where they **are crushed**.*
- Карты (было → стало): Past Simple / Present Perfect пассив: *The trees **were cut down** and a car park **was built**. The school **has been extended**.*
- План на будущее: *A new road **will be constructed**. The shops **are going to be replaced** by a supermarket.*
### Task 2
- Безличный пассив для «общего мнения»: ***It is often argued that** … / Fast food **is widely believed to** contribute to obesity.*
- Модальный пассив для решений: *Stricter laws **should be introduced**. More money **could be invested** in…*
- Не превращай всё в пассив: в своём мнении нужен актив (*I believe…*).
