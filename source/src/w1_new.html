Practice — Process: a natural cycle

The diagram below shows the life cycle of the salmon. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features. Write at least 150 words.
  1. Eggs — laid among small stones (gravel) in shallow water in the upper river.
  2. Alevin — tiny fish hatch after about 3 months; stay hidden in the gravel for about 6 weeks, feeding on a yolk sac.
  3. Fry — swim freely in the river and feed on insects for up to 1 year.
  4. Smolt — 12–15 cm long; travel downstream to the sea.
  5. Adult salmon — live in the ocean for 1–4 years; grow to 70–80 cm.
  6. Return — swim back up the river where they hatched and lay eggs → the cycle starts again.

Practice — Process: manufacturing / recycling

The diagram below shows the process by which used glass bottles are recycled. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features. Write at least 150 words.
  1. Collection — used bottles are left in bottle banks and collected
  2. Transport — by lorry to a recycling plant
  3. Cleaning — washed with high-pressure water
  4. Sorting — by colour: green, brown, clear
  5. Crushing — into small pieces (“cullet”)
  6. Melting — cullet + sand, soda ash, limestone; furnace at 1,500°C
  7. Moulding — liquid glass poured into moulds; new bottles cool
  8. Filling & delivery — filled with drinks, sent to shops, bought by consumers

Подсказка Simon: в overview — число этапов, начало и конец, и то, что процесс цикличный. Пассив почти везде: are collected, is crushed, is poured. Раздели этапы на два абзаца (например, 1–5 и 6–8).

Practice — Diagram: how a system works

The diagram below shows how a pumped-storage hydroelectric power station operates during the day and at night. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features. Write at least 150 words.

Day — electricity is produced

  1. Water held behind a dam in a high-level reservoir
  2. Intake gates open; water flows down a large pipe
  3. Water drives reversible turbines in the power station
  4. Turbines turn generators → electricity
  5. Power lines carry electricity to the national grid; water flows into a low-level reservoir

Night — water is pumped back

  1. Demand for electricity is low; cheap power from the grid
  2. Turbines run in reverse and work as pumps
  3. Water pumped from the low-level reservoir back up to the high-level reservoir
  4. Ready for use the next day

Это не «процесс производства», а схема работы устройства. Структура та же: overview — две фазы (день и ночь), затем по абзацу на каждую.
